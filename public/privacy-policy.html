This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile app and website (together, the “Service”). The Service lets you create chores, place bets using in-app virtual currency, view leaderboards, and purchase virtual rewards. The Service is operated by the developer identified on our Google Play store listing (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Summary: We collect account and gameplay information needed to run the app. We use Google Firebase for authentication and cloud storage. We do not sell your personal information. All betting uses fictional in-app currency only — not real money.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Service, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: email address, password (stored and processed by Firebase Authentication; we do not store your plain-text password), and a display name (“handle”) you choose at sign-up.
  • Gameplay and profile data: virtual wallet balance, chore titles and deadlines, completion status, bets you place (side, stake, outcome), leaderboard ranking, virtual rewards you create or redeem, and timestamps related to this activity.
  • Identifiers: a Firebase user ID used to associate your account with your data.
  • Technical data: basic connection and device information processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers (for example, IP address, browser or WebView type, and app version) when you access the Service. Our Android app requests Internet access only and does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

We do not knowingly collect government ID numbers, payment card numbers, precise geolocation, photos, or health information.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide core features: chores, betting, settlement, leaderboards, and the reward store
  • Maintain your virtual wallet and transaction history
  • Display your handle and balance on leaderboards visible to other signed-in users
  • Protect the security and integrity of the Service
  • Respond to support requests and enforce our terms
  • Comply with applicable law

We do not use your information for real-money gambling, credit decisions, or targeted advertising based on sensitive categories.

3. Virtual Currency

All currency in the Service is fictional and has no real-world cash value. You cannot deposit, withdraw, or exchange in-app currency for real money or prizes of monetary value through the Service.

We share information only in these circumstances:

  • With other users: your display name and virtual balance may appear on leaderboards. Chore titles and betting pools may be visible to other signed-in users as part of gameplay.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to host accounts and app data. Firebase processes data on our behalf according to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not sell your personal information.
